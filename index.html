Schnoeddel Tech Uploader

Schnoeddel Tech Uploader is a private publishing tool used by the owner of the Schnoeddel Tech YouTube channel.

It uploads the channel's own explainer videos and Shorts to YouTube and sets their title, description, tags, thumbnail, visibility and related settings, using the YouTube Data API. It acts only on the channel owner's own YouTube channel, after the owner signs in with Google and grants access.

The tool is not offered to the public, has no other users, and does not access anyone else's YouTube or Google account.

Uses YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service.
